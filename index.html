About HaulIntel

Built for company drivers and owner-operators who are tired of recruiter spin.

What this is

HaulIntel is a free research tool for CDL drivers. You type a carrier name and get a briefing that covers the things that actually matter when you’re choosing a seat: whether the pay matches the promise, how often you get home, what the trucks look like, how dispatch treats you, safety/FMCSA signals, and what other drivers are saying right now.

How it works

  1. Search — Enter a company name from the home page or Research section.
  2. Briefing — Review structured notes on pay, home time, equipment, management, safety, and sentiment.
  3. Chat — Ask follow-up questions in plain language (“Is the CPM after 2,500 miles?”).

Demo vs. live

This site is a working demo with high-quality sample data. It shows exactly how the product will feel. Live research powered by Grok — real-time synthesis of public records, reviews, and industry signals — is coming next. Nothing here is legal, employment, or financial advice. Always verify with the carrier, your contract, and official sources like SAFER/FMCSA.

Who it’s for

  • Company drivers comparing fleets before orientation
  • Owner-operators checking brokers and lease-purchase programs
  • Anyone who’s been burned by “home weekly” that meant every 6 weeks

Roadmap

  • ✓ Static demo (this site)
  • ○ Live Grok-powered briefings via API
  • ○ DOT / MC number lookup
  • ○ Save & compare carriers on your phone
